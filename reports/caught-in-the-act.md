# Caught In The Act: A Mechanistic Approach To Detecting Deception

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19505)

The paper studies linear probes of residual-stream activations for detecting deceptive arguments. Its comparisons include language-model judges and iterative nullspace projection. The reported model families include Qwen2.5 and DeepSeek-R1.

## Release Audit

A matching author implementation was not confirmed during the recorded audit. The data-generation procedure, instruction filtering and dependency versions therefore remain unverified.

## Replication Status

No baseline experiment was completed and no replication metric is available. The study contributes an artifact-access audit. It does not provide evidence for or against the paper's empirical claim or cross-model generalization.
